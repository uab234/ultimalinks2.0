Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trading safely across the China-Africa commercial corridor.

Our China-based audit team conducts mandatory government registry verification (AIC business license), on-site physical supplier inspections, machinery capacity audits, export license verification, and reviews past international fulfillment track records.

When an African buyer places an order, payment is deposited into an internationally monitored escrow account. The supplier is notified that funds are secured and commences production. Payout to the supplier occurs only after our on-site inspection team confirms quality compliance and issue the bill of lading.

We support international wire transfers in USD, NGN, EUR, and GBP, local African banking settlements via approved financial institutions, and payout to Chinese suppliers in both USD and CNY (Chinese Yuan).

Shipping individual LCL orders directly from multiple factories is expensive due to separate port handling, export documents, and customs clearance fees. We receive all your orders at our designated China hub, consolidate them into one container, and clear them as a unified shipment, saving up to 40% in logistics costs.

If goods fail to meet the approved sample specifications or contract parameters during our pre-shipment audit in China, funds remain locked in escrow. The supplier must correct the defects or replace the batch before dispatch; otherwise, the buyer receives a full escrow refund.
